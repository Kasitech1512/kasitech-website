Tallyroo is open-source, self-hosted bookkeeping for Australian small businesses and ABN holders who prepare their own quarterly BAS.

Meet Tallyroo
In our portfolio

Bookkeeping that stays in your hands.

Record income and expenses, import bank statements, organise transactions with rules, store supporting documents and prepare quarterly BAS worksheets. Tallyroo keeps business data on your own server and includes exports for your records or accountant.

Self-hosted bookkeeping

Tallyroo

Built for Australian small businesses and ABN holders managing their own books. Import CSV, OFX/QFX, QIF or PDF statements; categorise transactions; attach receipts; prepare BAS summaries; and export reports and supporting documents.

Tallyroo does not lodge with the ATO or provide tax advice. BAS figures have not been accountant-reviewed; check calculations with your accountant or the ATO before lodging.
