Family meal planner

TiffinPal

Plan children’s school lunch boxes for the week from a recipe library. Set child-specific portions, keep declared allergens out of plans, and get a shopping list grouped by supermarket aisle with a budget estimate.

Planning estimates only—not medical or dietary advice. Always check ingredient labels for allergens.

Personalised story app

StoryNest

Create illustrated bedtime stories starring your child, inspired by their profile, interests and events from their day. Choose a story style and length, then read aloud together or revisit stories in the library.

Profiles and story records are stored on the device. Optional AI image generation depends on the configured image service.
